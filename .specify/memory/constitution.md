# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course Constitution

## Core Principles

### Technical Accuracy and Verification
All content must maintain high technical accuracy based on verified primary sources. Every factual claim must be supported by credible evidence and peer-reviewed research. Theoretical concepts and practical implementations must be grounded in established scientific literature and validated methodologies.

### Accessibility and Clarity
Content must be written with clear, accessible language for CS/robotics learners. Complex topics should be broken down into digestible explanations and structured learning paths. The writing should facilitate understanding for readers with varying levels of technical background in AI and robotics.

### Reproducible Learning Materials
All explanations, algorithms, and examples must be reproducible. Code examples, simulations, and practical exercises must be tested and verifiable. This ensures learners can follow along and validate their understanding through hands-on practice.

### Academic Rigor and Quality
Content must adhere to academic standards with preference for peer-reviewed references. The material should reflect current research and industry best practices. Quality is maintained through rigorous fact-checking and validation of all technical claims.

### Ethical Standards and Citation Integrity
Zero tolerance for plagiarism. All sources must be properly cited using APA 7th edition format. At least 50% of sources must be peer-reviewed (IEEE, ACM, academic journals). All borrowed ideas, data, and concepts must be appropriately attributed.

### Practical Application Focus
Content must bridge theoretical knowledge with practical implementation. Each concept should be accompanied by relevant examples, case studies, or algorithms that demonstrate real-world application in physical AI and humanoid robotics systems.

## Quality Standards

All factual claims must be cited using APA 7th edition format. At least 50% of sources must be peer-reviewed publications from IEEE, ACM, or academic journals. The writing level should be between Flesch-Kincaid Grade 10-12 to ensure accessibility while maintaining technical precision. All code examples must be tested and reproducible before inclusion in the course material.

## Development Constraints

The total book length must remain between 20,000-30,000 words to ensure comprehensive yet focused coverage. A minimum of 20 credible sources must be included to support the content. No unverifiable or speculative claims are allowed. All chapters must export cleanly to PDF format with proper citations and formatting maintained.

## Development Workflow

Use /sp.chapter, /sp.section, /sp.topic, /sp.sources for all content creation. Every chapter must include validated citations and facts. Content must be compatible with Docusaurus for web deployment and GitHub Pages hosting. The development process must ensure smooth Docusaurus build and GitHub Pages deployment with reviewer-confirmed clarity and correctness.

## Governance

This constitution supersedes all other practices within the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Course project. All amendments require proper documentation and approval following the established versioning policy. All content must verify compliance with these principles during review processes.

All PRs and reviews must verify compliance with technical accuracy, citation standards, and reproducibility requirements. Complexity must be justified with clear educational value. Use documentation templates for structured development guidance.
