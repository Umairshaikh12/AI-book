## Sensor Layer
- **LiDAR**: Provides 360-degree range measurements for obstacle detection and mapping
- **IMU**: Measures acceleration, angular velocity, and orientation for balance and motion estimation
- **Camera**: Provides visual information for object recognition, depth estimation, and scene understanding

## Processing Layer
- **SLAM**: Simultaneous Localization and Mapping - builds a map of the environment while tracking robot position
- **Object Detection**: Identifies and classifies objects in the environment
- **Motion Planner**: Plans safe and efficient paths around obstacles

## Decision & Control Layer
- **Fusion & Integration**: Combines data from multiple sensors to create a coherent understanding of the environment
- **Mapping**: Creates global and local maps of the environment
- **Localization**: Determines the robot's position and orientation in the environment
- **Obstacle Avoidance**: Detects and avoids both static and dynamic obstacles
- **Control & Actuation**: Converts high-level navigation commands to low-level motor commands

## Key Considerations for Humanoid Robots
- Balance and stability constraints during locomotion
- Integration with bipedal walking controllers
- Handling of uneven terrain and obstacles
- Coordination between perception and balance systems
